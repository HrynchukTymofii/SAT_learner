Android icon shapes

Android launchers crop the icon to their own shape: a circle on many phones, a rounded square of about 35 to 40% on older Samsung, Xiaomi and similar, or a squircle on Pixel. Here the curve still runs off the edges and SAT is 95% of the iPhone size and sits a little lower-left, so the circle doesn't cut the T. Sizes: 192, 96 and 48 px.
