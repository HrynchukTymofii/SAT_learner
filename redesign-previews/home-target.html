What to check on your phone

  1. Tab bar runs the full width of the screen (16 pt gap each side), floating above the home indicator. Each tab is a quarter of the bar, and the active one has a wide white pill.
  2. Every card is full width (screen minus 20 pt on each side): the butter "continue" card, the week strip, the task rows and the two promo cards.
  3. Pastel cards have rounded corners, a faint graph-paper grid, and a drawing on the right that stays inside the card.
  4. Chip row and recent chapters scroll sideways and run to the screen edge.
  5. Headings use Outfit, everything else uses Manrope.
